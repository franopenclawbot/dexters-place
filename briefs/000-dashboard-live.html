What this is

This is the desk dashboard: daily briefs, watchlist snapshots and desk status will publish here once the data pipeline is live. It is deliberately unstyled for now — structure first, polish later.

Live today

  • Nginx container dexter-dashboard serving on port 8080 (LAN/private network)
  • Files are bind-mounted, not baked in — a new brief publishes the moment it is saved to briefs/ and added to briefs/index.json
  • Brief archive wired to the manifest; no build step anywhere

Pending

  • Market data providers (FRED, SEC EDGAR, quote API) — the first data-backed brief lands after this; figures will be sourced and timestamped, never recalled from memory
  • Operator onboarding: objectives, holdings, risk budget, benchmark
  • Scheduled cadence in America/New_York: 07:00 / 09:15 / 12:00 / 16:30 weekday briefs

Standing rule

Nothing publishes unverified. No forecast is stated without assumptions. And no private data — holdings, journals, transactions — ever goes on a public repo or page.
